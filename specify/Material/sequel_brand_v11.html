01Brand story

Every layer tells a different story.

The safest-looking model in isolation can be dangerous three layers down. Sequel tests all the way through — from what a model says, to what it becomes when connected, to how it behaves inside systems at scale.

The logo is a geological cross-section: the word "sequel" cut through four strata, each one a level of evaluation. A vertical ember probe marks the point of measurement. The deeper you go, the more you find.

09 Principles

Design principles

One rule per level of abstraction.

L1
Start at the surface

The bone page is legible, calm, warm. Complexity is introduced layer by layer — never all at once. The green cover signals depth before you've read a word.

L2
The dot reveals everything

The ember probe dot is the only saturated element on the light palette. That scarcity is the point. It always means: this is where we're measuring. Never add a second.

L3
Green carries authority

Forest green is reserved for the cover, dark surfaces, card headers, and the grove button. It signals depth and seriousness. Never use it as a decorative tint on the light surface.

L4
Failure is structural, not exceptional

Ember marks both the probe and the fail state. Finding failure is the product. Show it with the same calm authority as a pass — never with alarm or drama.
